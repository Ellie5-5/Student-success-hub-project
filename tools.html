📊 Study Time Cheat Sheet

Not sure how much time you should spend studying each week outside of class? Here is the general rule of thumb:


Light Semester (9 – 12 Credit Hours)

Plan for roughly 18 – 24 hours of independent study time per week.

Full-Time Semester (15 Credit Hours)

Plan for roughly 30 hours of independent study time per week.

Heavy Semester (18+ Credit Hours)

Plan for roughly 36+ hours of independent study time per week.
